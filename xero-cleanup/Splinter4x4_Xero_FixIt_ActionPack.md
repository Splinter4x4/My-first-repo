# 4. SETUP GUIDE — turn on automated invoice reminders

1. In Xero, click your **organisation name** → **Settings**.
2. Under *Features*, choose **Invoice settings**.
3. Click **Reminders** (top right).
4. Tick **Email reminders are turned on**.
5. Set reminders for e.g. **7, 14 and 30 days overdue** (edit the wording if you like).
6. Optionally tick "only send if the amount is over $___".
7. **Save.** From now on Xero chases overdue invoices automatically.

---

# 5. SETUP GUIDE — track stock on hand (inventory items)

1. In Xero, go to **Business** → **Products and services**.
2. Click **New item** for each stock product (e.g. fridges, drawer kits, Voltek parts).
3. Tick **"I track this item"** and set the inventory asset account.
4. Enter the cost & sale price.
5. From now on, buying stock adds to your **stock-on-hand value** and selling reduces it — so your dashboard can show what's actually sitting on the shelf, not just what you've spent.

> Note: items bought before you switch this on won't be counted — do an opening **stock take** and enter quantities to start accurate.
