About · Neerabup, WA

Built in Neerabup.
Made for the bush.

Splinter 4X4 builds ultra-lightweight touring storage for people who actually use it — composite drawers, bolt-in floors and 12V that come back the same shape they went out.

See the range →Custom quote

We started Splinter because the storage going into touring 4WDs was too heavy. Steel drawers eat the payload you need for water, fuel, recovery gear and the rest of the kit that keeps a trip running.

So we build in composite. The systems are lighter, they bolt to factory points, and they're laid out around how people actually pack — fridge slides that clear the tailgate, tables where they help, drawers that don't rattle loose on corrugations.

Everything's designed and built at our Neerabup workshop in WA, and shaken down off-grid before it ships. Supply it yourself anywhere in Australia, or have us fit it.
