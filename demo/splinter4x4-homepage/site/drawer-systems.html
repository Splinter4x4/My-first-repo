Why composite drawers

01

Composite, not steel

Built from composite panels, so a full system saves serious weight over steel — weight you get back as payload for water, fuel and recovery gear.

02

Fridge that clears the tailgate

Slides and hutches are laid out so your fridge opens clear of the tailgate, with a pull-out table where it actually helps.

03

Supply or installed

Order supply-only and fit it yourself, or have it installed at our Neerabup workshop. Wagon or ute-tub configurations.

Common questions

What's the lead time?
Most drawer systems are 2–6 weeks from order to dispatch or install, depending on the model and how busy the workshop is.
Wagon or ute — what's the difference?
Wagon systems suit 4-door 4WDs and SUVs; ute versions are built for tub setups and are priced a little higher. You choose on the product page.
Which fridge can I run?
Sandy Cape comes with an Evakool 40L or Engel 30L supplied, or we build to suit a fridge you already own. Other systems can be specced to suit.
Can you install it?
Yes — installation is available at our Neerabup, WA workshop. Supply-only orders ship Australia-wide.
