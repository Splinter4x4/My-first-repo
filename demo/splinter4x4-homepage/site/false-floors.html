Common questions

Do I need to drill anything?
No. The floors bolt into factory mounting points. It's a clean, reversible install.
What are the access panels for?
They let you reach the spare, tools or storage under the floor. Pick the layout that matches how you pack on the product page.
How heavy are they?
Light — the Everest floor is around 20 kg and the Prado 250 around 30 kg, depending on options.
Can you fit a vehicle that's not listed?
Often yes — send us your vehicle through a custom quote and we'll let you know.
