About · Neerabup, WA

Built in the West.
Tested where you tour.

Splinter 4X4 started because the storage going into touring rigs was too heavy. Steel drawers eat the payload you need for water, fuel and recovery gear — so we build in composite.

Every system is designed, engineered and built at our Neerabup workshop. False floors bolt to factory mounting points — no drilling, no rattles. Drawer systems arrive fully constructed, ready to drop in, or we install them here.

12V is done properly too — supplied and installed by Matt at Voltek Auto Electrical and integrated into the build, so power and storage go in together, once.

Supply-only orders ship Australia-wide, fully constructed, with $400 off RRP. Typical lead time is 2–4 weeks.
